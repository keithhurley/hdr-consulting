Featured Product: BloodHawk™

Flagship Android App
BLOODHAWK™ Optical Wildlife Recovery
Download APK Release →

BloodHawk™ is a professional supplementary optical tool designed for ethical hunters and wildlife trackers. It combines multi-stage color space segmentation (HSV & LAB) with real-time video simulation and diagnostic hit-site analysis to aid in post-shot game recovery.

  • ✓
    100% On-Device Processing: Camera analysis runs entirely on local handset silicon. Camera images and private coordinates never leave your device.
  • ✓
    Native C++ Engine: Custom high-performance OpenCV native pipeline optimized for multi-core ARM64 architectures.
  • ✓
    Offline Topo Cartography: Built-in regional map tile caching for navigating dense canopy and remote public/private lands without cellular coverage.
  • ✓
    Diagnostic Hit Advisor: Integrated veterinary sign reference assisting trackers in assessing vital sign characteristics and tracking delay strategy.
