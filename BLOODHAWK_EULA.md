## 3. Field Safety, Operational Limitations & Inherent Risks

### 3.1 Supplementary Aid Only
BloodHawk™ is designed to assist users in visually scanning for possible blood sign and referencing terrain during lawful game recovery. It is **not** a certified navigation, survival, or emergency tool. Always carry standard wilderness essentials, including a physical magnetic compass, paper maps, reliable lighting, weather-appropriate gear, and an emergency communication device.

### 3.2 Probabilistic Detection & Technical Limitations
- **False Positives:** Natural materials (such as fallen leaves, berries, bark, red clay, lichen, or rust) may trigger optical alerts, visual reticles, haptic pulses, or audio signals.
- **False Negatives:** Blood sign may fail to register due to oxidation, rain, snow, heavy duff, shadows, motion blur, torch light characteristics, or distance from the target.
- **No Guarantee:** HDRC makes no warranty or guarantee that the App will detect blood, maintain a continuous trail, or result in the recovery of any animal.
- **Sensors & Battery:** GPS accuracy varies with forest canopy, terrain, and weather. Compass sensors are subject to magnetic interference from firearms, bows, and metallic gear. Extended camera and screen use, particularly in cold temperatures, can drain device batteries rapidly.

### 3.3 Inherent Outdoor Risks & Express Assumption of Risk
Outdoor activities, hunting, and tracking wild game carry inherent risks, including rough terrain, falls, extreme weather, hypothermia, water hazards, weapon accidents, and encounters with wounded or dangerous wildlife. Never look at your device while walking or navigating obstacles. Maintain constant situational awareness and stable footing.
