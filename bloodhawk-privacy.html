Core Privacy Commitment

Your Data Stays on Your Handset

BloodHawk™ is an on-device field tool designed to assist hunters in tracking blood trails and recovering game.

  • On-Device Architecture: Camera imagery is analyzed in real time and saved recovery trails, custom profiles, and settings are stored locally on your handset.
  • No Developer Servers or Tracking: Hurley Data and Research Consulting LLC does not operate remote user servers in conjunction with and use with BloodHawk, does not maintain user accounts, and does not collect or receive your camera frames, photos, GPS trails, or telemetry.
  • No Sale or Marketing: Hurley Data and Research Consulting LLC does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for commercial, marketing, or advertising purposes.
Section 2

On-Device Processing & Data Storage

  • Live Camera Scanning (Temporary RAM Only): When you scan with optical blood tracking, camera frames are analyzed in temporary device memory (RAM) in real time to highlight blood droplets. Live camera frames are discarded from memory after rendering and are never recorded to disk or transmitted over the network.
  • Calibration & Hit Diagnostic Photos: When you freeze a camera frame to calibrate a blood profile or take a photo in Hit Site Diagnostic, the image is held only in temporary RAM while that screen is active. BloodHawk does not save calibration or diagnostic photos to storage—only your numeric color bounds, profile labels, notes, and timestamps are saved in local app storage.
  • Foreground GPS & Trail Plotting: BloodHawk accesses your device’s GPS location only while the application is actively open in the foreground. Location updates stop immediately when you pause tracking, switch away from the app, or lock your device screen. Saved waypoints, custom field markers, and path coordinates remain in private local storage on your handset.
  • User-Initiated Exports & Sharing: If you choose to export or share a recovery trail or custom profile (.bhawk, .gpx, .geojson, .kml, .json), BloodHawk generates an unencrypted file and passes it to the Android share sheet or system file picker. Trail exports can contain location coordinates, timestamps, names, and notes, depending on the selected format. Any subsequent storage, transmission, or cloud sync is governed by the destination app or service you choose (such as Google Drive, email, or a messaging app).
  • Data Security & Device Sandboxing: On-device data is protected by standard Android operating-system application sandboxing. BloodHawk does not independently apply proprietary encryption to internal database files or exported files. You are responsible for maintaining your device's passcode, lock screen, and physical security.
  • Local Retention & Deletion Scope: Saved recovery sessions, custom profiles, and cached map tiles remain in private app storage until you delete them inside the app or uninstall BloodHawk. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored within the app’s private sandbox; previously exported files or shared copies must be deleted separately where they were saved.
Section 3

Network Connections & Third-Party Services

Blood tracking and saved-trail features work offline. Map imagery requires previously downloaded or cached tiles, or an internet connection. Network connections occur only in the following specific circumstances:

3.1 Cartographic Map Tile Providers

When you view an online map layer or download an offline map pack, your handset connects directly via HTTPS to public map tile services:

  • U.S. Geological Survey (USGS) National Map (basemap.nationalmap.gov): Serves public domain aerial/satellite orthoimagery (USGSImageryOnly) and topographic contour (USGSTopo) tiles for map viewing and bulk offline pack downloads. (USGS Privacy Policy)
  • OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org): Serves community map tiles during interactive viewing. Viewed tiles are cached locally on your device to prevent repeat network requests. (OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy)

Information Transmitted: Map tile requests include the requested tile coordinates (zoom/x/y), your device’s IP address, timestamp, and standard HTTPS headers identifying BloodHawk. Map tile coordinates indicate the geographic area currently displayed on your screen. Map providers do not receive your blood drop pins, trail names, notes, or custom markers.

Disabling Map Connections: You can prevent all map tile network requests by selecting the Offline Coordinate Grid (GRID) map layer (which makes zero network requests) or by disabling mobile data and Wi-Fi on your device. Pre-downloading USGS Offline Map Packs also provides local tile storage for your hunting area.

Third-Party Provider Disclaimer: Hurley Data and Research Consulting LLC does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices, data retention, or security of third-party map providers (USGS or OpenStreetMap Foundation).

3.2 App Distribution, Diagnostics & Updates

When distributed through the Google Play Store, app installation, updates, and platform diagnostics are managed natively by Google LLC under the Google Privacy Policy. Google may provide aggregated, de-identified crash logs and performance metrics through the Google Play Console to help us identify and fix software bugs. HDRC does not receive identifying user telemetry from these reports.

Section 4

Hardware Permissions & Sensor Use

BloodHawk requests only the device permissions strictly necessary for its field tools:

Permission / Sensor Purpose in BloodHawk Network Transmission
Camera (CAMERA) Optical detection of blood droplets on the ground and LED torch control. None. Frames are processed in temporary RAM and discarded immediately.
Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) Foreground plotting of your position, blood drop pins, and compass vectors. None to Developer. Saved trails remain local. Online map tiles request geographic grid squares from map servers as described in Section 3.
Internet (INTERNET) Fetching public map tiles when online map layers are enabled. Transmits standard HTTPS request data to map servers (IP address and requested tile coordinates).
Vibration (VIBRATE) Haptic pulse alerts when blood droplets are detected. None. Local hardware actuator only.
Compass / Motion Sensors (Magnetometer & Rotation Vector) Rendering 360° compass heading on the tracking HUD and map. Processed locally; saved headings may be included in trail exports.
